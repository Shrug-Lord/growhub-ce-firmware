# Growhub Remote Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 — Plongee6 LLC

Growhub Remote does not collect data for its developer. It contains no accounts,
advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated cloud service.

## Local network and saved controllers

The app uses Local Network permission to discover compatible Growhub CE controllers
and exchange status and control requests directly with them. Controller hardware
IDs, network addresses and display names are saved on your device so the app can
remember them. Outlet names are the equipment labels saved directly on your
controller and cached on your phone; changes are shared with its other local
controls. They are not uploaded to us. Native status readings are held in
memory. Your controller's own behavior is governed by its installed firmware.

## Full controls and external services

Full controls uses native screens to read settings and send commands directly to
your controller. With older CE firmware, its settings page is parsed as data;
the app does not display it or execute its scripts. Configuration is held in
memory; Wi-Fi passwords you enter are sent only to the selected controller and
are not retained as app settings. Firmware updates may contact the sources you
choose. External websites and email services follow their own policies.

Earlier app builds used an embedded controller page that could retain local
website data. Deleting the app removes any website data left by those builds.

## Your choices

You can deny Local Network permission or use the hardware-free demo. Swipe left
on a controller to forget its saved identity and address. Deleting the app removes
its local data, including embedded website data. This does not erase settings on
the controller itself. Device backups follow your Apple backup settings.

Support email includes only information you choose to send. We use it to respond
to your request. Please avoid including Wi-Fi passwords or other secrets.
